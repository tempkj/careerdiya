Privacy Policy

How we look after your information

Last updated: [DD Month 2026]

Career Diya is a career guidance service operated by ASKRJ Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. ("Career Diya", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use www.careerdiya.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the details you add to your profile, such as your name, stage (student, graduate or professional), education and work experience.
  • Résumé content: if you choose to import a résumé, the file stays in your browser. The text is extracted there and may be sent to our AI service to identify profile fields; it is not kept after that. Only the profile details you review and save are stored.
  • Exploration answers and results: your answers to the career exploration questions, the directions suggested to you, and the careers or courses you save.
  • Enquiries: details you send through our counselling, admissions or course-interest forms, such as your name, phone number, email and the help you are looking for.
  • Device storage: we keep a few items in your browser's local storage, such as your sign-in session and unsaved progress, so the site works smoothly. We do not use advertising cookies.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your Career Diya account.
  • To suggest career directions, paths and courses that fit your answers and profile.
  • To respond to your enquiries and arrange counselling sessions you ask for.
  • To improve our guidance and fix problems with the site.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Children and young people

Many of our users are students. If you are under 18, please use Career Diya with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian, and ask them to read this policy with you. Where the law requires verifiable parental consent, we will ask for it before processing a minor's personal data. Parents and guardians can contact us at any time to review or delete their child's information.

4. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who host our website and database, and who provide the AI services we use to generate personalised guidance. They process data only on our instructions.
  • Career counsellors working with Career Diya, only when you ask for a counselling session.
  • Assessment partner: our deeper psychometric assessment is provided on a partner website. Anything you enter there is covered by that provider's own privacy policy.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active, or as long as we need it to provide the service you asked for. When you delete your account or ask us to erase your data, we delete it unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and you can nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. To do any of this, write to info@careerdiya.com.

7. Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

8. Questions and complaints

Grievance Officer: [Name], ASKRJ Enterprises Pvt. Ltd., [Registered address], info@careerdiya.com. We will respond to grievances within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will also tell you when you next sign in.
