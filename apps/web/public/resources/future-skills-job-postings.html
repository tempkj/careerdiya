The Career Skills You Need Tomorrow May Already Be Showing Up in Job Postings Today

How job postings can provide early signals about changing skill requirements.

Career Planning 5 mi read

One useful idea in Lightcast's report is that labour-market data can provide an early signal of changing skill requirements.

Lightcast analysed more than 43 million online job postings from 2021–2024 and more than 32,000 skills in its skills library for this report.

Why job postings matter

A qualification tells you what someone has studied.

A job posting tells you what an employer is asking for right now.

Those aren't always the same thing.

Skills can reveal change before job titles do

A job title may remain unchanged for years while the skills attached to it change much faster.

Emerging skills can therefore act as an early signal of where a career is heading.

Lightcast found strong growth in several AI-related skills and increasing green-skill requirements across occupations.

Turn career planning into a habit

Instead of asking every few years:

"Is my career still relevant?"

try:

Explore → Check demand → Compare skills → Identify gaps → Learn → Reassess

Career Diya takeaway

The future of work doesn't arrive overnight.

It leaves signals.

Job postings, emerging skills and changing employer requirements are some of those signals.
