The Fastest-Changing Careers Aren't Necessarily the Worst Careers

How to interpret career disruption without confusing change with decline.

Career Decisions 5 mi read

Would you avoid a career because its skills are changing quickly?

A highly disrupted career can sound risky. But Lightcast's Skills Disruption Index measures something more specific: how much the skills requested for occupations changed between 2021 and 2024.

Among the highly disrupted occupations in the report are Mobile Applications Developer, Data Engineer, Validation Engineer, Product Owner, Software QA Engineer / Tester and Database Administrator.

Disruption isn't the same as decline

A high disruption score means:

The skills associated with the occupation are changing significantly.

It does not automatically mean the occupation is disappearing.

Rapid change can mean that professionals need to adapt their skills.

Think of disruption as a signal

Ask:

  • What skills are growing?
  • Which skills are declining?
  • Which technologies are changing the work?
  • What adjacent skills are becoming valuable?
  • What skills could make me more adaptable?

Career Diya takeaway

Don't interpret career disruption simply as "dangerous."

Think:

Changing

Then investigate what the change actually means.
