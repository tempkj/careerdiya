Green Jobs Are Not the Only Green Careers

How sustainability skills are changing existing occupations.

Future of Work 4 mi read

When people hear "green jobs", they may think of renewable energy, sustainability or environmental science.

But the transition to a greener economy is also changing existing careers.

Lightcast describes this as the "greening" of existing roles.

Existing jobs are acquiring green skills

The report found green skills appearing in occupations that aren't traditionally considered green.

Project Managers, for example, may encounter requirements involving environmental law and policy, waste management and renewable energy. Mechanical engineering and safety roles can also acquire green requirements.

Two ways to participate in the green economy

Enter a green occupation

Examples include Sustainability Specialist, Environmental Scientist and Energy Engineer.

Green your existing career

A Project Manager, Engineer, Construction professional or Safety professional can remain in the same broad occupation while adding sustainability-related capabilities.

This second route is easy to overlook.

Why this matters

The future may not always be:

Old job → new green job

It can also be:

Existing job → existing job + green skills

Career Diya takeaway

Don't search only for jobs with "green" in the title.

Ask:

Which skills are being added to the careers I am already considering?

Sometimes the future opportunity is hidden inside an existing occupation.
