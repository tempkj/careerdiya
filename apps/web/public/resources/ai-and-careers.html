AI Isn't Just Creating AI Jobs: How Artificial Intelligence Is Changing Careers

How artificial intelligence is becoming part of many existing careers.

AI & Careers 5 mi read

Think AI is only for programmers?

The evidence in Lightcast's UK Skills Revolution report shows a broader picture.

AI-related skills grew substantially between 2014 and 2024. Generative-AI mentions in UK job postings increased from roughly 500 in 2014 to around 17,000 in 2024.

More importantly, AI demand is appearing outside traditional technology roles.

AI is entering non-technology careers

Lightcast identifies occupations such as Researcher / Research Associate, Compliance Manager, Marketing Manager, Sales Representative, Writer, Translator, Biologist, Product Manager and Business / Management Analyst alongside technology roles requesting AI skills.

You don't necessarily need to become an AI engineer to benefit from AI.

AI can become a layer on top of your existing career

A marketer can use AI for research, content and campaign optimisation.

A researcher can use AI to assist with large datasets and insight generation.

A sales professional can use AI for lead generation, customer research and personalisation.

These people haven't necessarily moved into AI careers. They have become AI-enabled professionals.

The career advantage

A useful combination may increasingly be:

Domain expertise + digital capability + AI fluency

Your existing experience can be an advantage because you already understand your customers, processes, problems and industry.

Should you switch careers because of AI?

Before making a dramatic career switch, ask:

Can AI make me better at the career I already have?

Then ask:

Are there adjacent careers where my existing experience becomes more valuable when combined with AI skills?

Career Diya takeaway

Don't ask only, "What AI jobs are growing?"

Also ask:

"How is AI changing the career I already have?"
