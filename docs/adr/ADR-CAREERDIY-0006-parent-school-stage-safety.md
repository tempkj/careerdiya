# ADR-CAREERDIY-0006: Parent / School-Stage Safety and Language

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

Parents may use Career Diya to explore options for children. This is the highest-risk audience for overclaiming because the adult RIASEC-42 instrument is not the instrument for younger children.

## Decision

Parent questions are answered **by the parent about the child**.

### Allowed framing

- “What kinds of activities does your child seem to enjoy most?”
- “Your responses suggest that technology and engineering may be worth exploring for your child.”
- “The answers you provided point toward an interest in problem-solving.”

### Prohibited framing

- “Your child is highly analytical.”
- “Your child is suited to engineering.”
- “Your child should become a software engineer.”
- personality/aptitude/psychometric conclusions about the child.

The parent free engine recommends broad direction families, not definitive occupations.

The parent CTA routes to the **co-branded age-designed school-stage assessment** when deeper evidence is desired. It must not route to RIASEC-42.

## Consequences

The result remains observational, exploratory and appropriately bounded for parent decision-making.
