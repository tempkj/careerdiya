# ADR-CAREERDIY-0003: Free Exploration vs Deeper/Paid Assessment

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

The free exploration must be valuable enough to reduce uncertainty without making the deeper assessment commercially or substantively redundant.

## Decision

The free experience answers:

> What broad directions are worth exploring next?

The deeper/paid assessment answers:

> What career directions fit me more deeply, and why?

### Free exploration may provide

- roughly seven lightweight questions;
- broad directions/families;
- 2-3 hypotheses to explore;
- plain-language rationale;
- practical experiments / next steps;
- an explicit invitation to obtain deeper evidence.

### Free exploration must not provide

- formal psychometric scores;
- definitive career-fit percentages;
- detailed personality/aptitude profiles;
- extensive occupational ranking;
- definitive child career conclusions;
- claims of validated psychometric methodology.

## Consequences

The free scoring engine can be sophisticated internally while the user-facing output remains intentionally bounded.
