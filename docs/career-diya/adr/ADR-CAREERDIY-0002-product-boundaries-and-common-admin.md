# ADR-CAREERDIY-0002: Product Boundaries and Common Admin

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

Career Diya and Skill Diya solve adjacent but different problems and should not become one consumer website.

## Decision

- **Career Diya:** career guidance, exploration, decisions, assessment pathways, counselling, career navigation.
- **Skill Diya:** practical skill development, practitioner-led learning, projects, assessments, evidence and certifications.
- **Swakojo Admin:** common operational/admin platform for both products.

Consumer websites, navigation, funnels and product experiences remain distinct.

Use contextual bridges rather than persistent cross-promotion of the sibling product.

## Consequences

Career Diya may recommend Skill Diya when a learning step is relevant, and Skill Diya may send uncertain users to Career Diya, without merging the consumer products.
