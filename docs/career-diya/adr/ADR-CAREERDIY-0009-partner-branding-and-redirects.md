# ADR-CAREERDIY-0009: Partner Branding and Redirects

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

Career Diya uses partner-provided specialist capability, but the consumer experience should remain Career Diya-branded.

## Decision

- Do not expose the name “Edumilestones” in normal user-facing Career Diya copy.
- Do not use “co-branded” or “partner experience” as navigation labels.
- Preserve underlying partner/deeper-assessment redirects and functionality.
- When a partner route remains the actual destination, label it according to the Career Diya capability being offered (for example, “Deeper assessment” or “School-stage assessment”).
- Do not fabricate direct API integrations or ownership claims.

## Consequences

The public journey feels like a coherent Career Diya product while specialist partner functionality remains reachable and technically attributable in implementation.
