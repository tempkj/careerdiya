# ADR-CAREERDIY-0011: Brand and UX System

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

Career Diya moved from a co-branded purple visual system to its own warm gold / indigo identity shared conceptually with Skill Diya.

## Decision

### Brand

- Primary/deep color: **#213A8F** (indigo)
- Warm accent: **#D4A017** (gold)
- Typeface: **Poppins**
- Warm paper/off-white background
- Black/gold CareerDiya logo on light surfaces
- White CareerDiya logo on indigo footer where required
- Do not retain stray legacy purple tokens or `#C99619`.

### UX

Career Diya should feel trustworthy, intelligent, calm, useful, non-judgmental, premium, practical and confidence-building.

Avoid exaggerated AI claims, guaranteed-career language, fear-based selling and generic EdTech styling.

The primary experience should help users feel that Career Diya is helping them figure something out, not simply selling a package.

## Consequences

Brand changes should normally be token-level and should not require component restructuring.
